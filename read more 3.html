A New Season 7 Unfinished Intro is Leaked!

A
By Alya Césaire
Senior Reporter @ The Ladyblog
📅 Published: August 8, 2026  •  ⏱️ 4 min read

Pound it, Miraculous fans! A rough animatic for the Season 7 opening intro recently surfaced, and it gives us an early look at what’s in store for the new season.

Here is what stands out from the clip:

  • Dual Voiceover: For the very first time, Adrien joins Marinette to speak the iconic opening lines ("In the daytime, I'm Marinette... In the daytime, I'm Adrien..."), sharing the intro duties right from the start.
  • Classic Character Beats: Early storyboard sketches feature Marinette and Adrien interacting, capturing their relationship dynamic before diving into the action.
  • Expanded Hero Roster: The animatic showcases a full lineup of the Miraculous heroes standing together, teasing the group poses and dynamic team chemistry we can expect.
  • Title Card Finish: The sequence wraps up with the familiar Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir logo layered over the storyboard sketches.

Work-in-progress storyboards often shift during production, so some camera angles or details could look different in the final release. What do you think about Adrien co-narrating the intro? Let us know in the comments!
